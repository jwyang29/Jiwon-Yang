Reading change, not level

The piece watches for difference rather than absolute value, so it never needs a room calibrated to it before use. Lean inside the hoop and your shadow steers; lift the pyramid and the load cell jumps. It reads change, not level. Built with Hyunseo Lee and Heekyeong Lee.
