The toy makes a sound, the web knows which toy it is

AUD:Silhouette is a fidget toy that collects sounds. Playing it near a device lets a web app capture the sound through the microphone, match it against the known signatures, and register that toy to the collection.

Putting the identification in the browser keeps the toy a toy. No microcontroller, no battery and no pairing have to live inside the shell, and a new toy joins the system by adding its signature rather than by changing the object.

VIB:Silhouette carries the same collecting idea to the palm instead of the ear. It is a design proposal: concept, form and CMF, modelled and rendered.
